Quarry Maker

Builds a true-scale 3D model of your quarry for Blender, Rhino and Gaea. Follow the four steps.

1

Your quarry

Find your quarry on the map, then draw a rectangle around it. Include some of the surroundings; an extra margin (500 m) is added on every side automatically.

2

Settings

Three things the program cannot guess.

Where is Blender on this computer?
Pick the detected Blender. If none is found, install Blender from blender.org, or choose "Other" and type its location (Mac: /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender, Windows: C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.2\blender.exe).
OpenTopography key
Needed to download the height data. Free at opentopography.org (My Account → Request API key). For your safety the key is only kept while this app is open; it is never saved to a file. You will be asked again next time.
Bench height
Quarry walls are cut as giant steps. The bench height is how tall one step is (usually 10–15 m).

The benches are carved into the terrain with walls of this height and flat floors between them. If you don't know it, an 8 m guess is used where the elevation data can't tell. Knowing it gives more accurate bench levels. Ask the quarry operator, look in the permit documents, or measure in Google Earth Pro: hover over one bench floor and the next one, and subtract the elevations shown at the bottom right.
3

Build the model

Run one step at a time and look at the check images after each step (recommended), or run everything at once. Downloads are kept, so running again is quick.
